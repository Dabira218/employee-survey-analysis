### Employee survey analysis

## Introduction:
Employee engagement and job satisfaction are important factors that influence workplace productivity and organizational effectiveness. This project analyzes employee survey data using Microsoft Excel to examine employees’ workplace experiences, perceptions of management, recognition, professional development, and relationships with colleagues. 

The aim is to identify patterns in employee feedback, highlight areas of strength and opportunities for improvement, and provide actionable recommendations to support a positive working environment.

## Key Metrics:
- Total survey responses: 14,575
- Survey questions analyzed: 10
- Overall average response score: Approximately 3.07

## Skills/Concepts Demonstrated:
-	Data cleaning and preparation
- Excel pivot tables and charts
-	Survey analysis and visualization
- Insight generation
- Data-driven recommendations

## Analysis:
- Most agreed questions: Q1-I know what is expected of me at work(1341 agreed), Q4 - My supervisor seems to care about me as a person(1246 agreed) indicating positive responses regarding clarity of work expectations.
- Most disagreed questions: Q6 -I have a best friend at work(606 disagreed), Q3- In the last seven days, I have received recognition or praise for doing good work (495), indicating negative responses regarding employee recognition and workplace relationships.
- Patterns by department exist. Most departments score between 3.0 and 3.6 on average. Only the Sheriff''s Department stands apart at an average response of 2.46.
- Employees know what is expected of them but feel less recognised for doing good work.
- Overall Job Satisfaction recorded an average score of 3.07, providing insight into employees’ overall satisfaction with their jobs.

## Conclusion:
The survey reveals positive perceptions of job expectations and supervisory support, while identifying opportunities to improve employee recognition, workplace relationships, and performance accountability.
# Recommendations:
-	Strengthen employee recognition and appreciation such as weekly thank you notes and shout-outs so good work is acknowledged regularly.
-	Run team building activities and mentoring pairs in order to promote teamwork and positive workplace relationships.
-	Expand learning and career development opportunities.
-	Improve performance feedback and accountability.
-	Continue promoting workplace inclusion and clear job expectations.
-	Monitor employee satisfaction through regular surveys.
